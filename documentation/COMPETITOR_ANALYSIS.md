## 2. Key Deficiencies in Existing Applications

1. **The Spot-Price Fallacy:**
   - Existing apps show today's tomato price (e.g. ₹50/kg). Farmers plant tomatoes, and 90 days later harvest into an extreme market glut (e.g. ₹6/kg).
   - **AgriShield Fix:** Explicit forward seasonal projections showing price drop probabilities around expected harvest dates.
2. **Post-Disaster Blind Spot:**
   - When a cyclone or heavy flood hits, existing apps provide zero assistance.
   - **AgriShield Fix:** Instant loss evidence collection, PMFBY 72-hour notification countdown, official loss dossier generation, and immediate link to FPO/KVK officers.
3. **The 30% Post-Harvest Wastage Dilemma:**
   - Cyclones and rainstorms leave tomatoes with minor skin cracks or water blemishes that regular retail mandis reject. Tons are dumped on highways.
   - **AgriShield Fix:** Module D connects farmers directly with puree, pulp, and sauce factories capable of processing Grade B tomatoes at fair floor prices.
